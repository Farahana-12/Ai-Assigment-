#  Sleep Health agent  

The Sleep Health Agent was developed to evaluate an individual's sleep quality using various health and lifestyle factors. The system analyzes important parameters such as Sleep Duration, Quality of Sleep, Stress Level, Physical Activity Level, and Heart Rate to calculate an overall Sleep Score.


## Parameters Used

* Sleep Duration
* Quality of Sleep
* Stress Level
* Physical Activity Level
* Heart Rate

## Sleep Score Calculation

The Sleep Score is calculated using weighted contributions from the selected health factors:

* Sleep Duration – 30%
* Quality of Sleep – 30%
* Stress Level – 15%
* Physical Activity Level – 15%
* Heart Rate – 10%

The calculated score ranges from 0 to 100.

## Sleep Category Classification

| Sleep Score Range | Category |
| ----------------- | -------- |
| 80 – 100          | Good     |
| 60 – 79           | Average  |
| Below 60          | Poor     |

## Analysis of Results

The agent  successfully calculated Sleep Scores for individuals using the selected health and lifestyle parameters. Higher scores were observed among individuals who maintained sufficient sleep duration, better sleep quality, regular physical activity, lower stress levels, and a healthy heart rate.

Individuals classified under the **Good** category demonstrated healthy sleeping habits and balanced lifestyle factors. Those in the **Average** category showed moderate sleep quality and may benefit from improvements in daily routines. Individuals classified as **Poor** require greater attention to sleep habits, stress management, and overall health practices.

The results indicate that sleep quality is strongly influenced by multiple lifestyle factors rather than a single parameter.
