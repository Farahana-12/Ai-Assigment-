# Simple Reflex Agent 

The agent was developed to analyze air quality data and determine the overall air quality level based on pollutant concentrations. The agent  processes environmental parameters and calculates AQI values, which are then classified into standard air quality categories for easier interpretation.

The dataset contains various air pollution indicators such as PM10, PM2.5, Carbon Monoxide, Nitrogen Dioxide, Sulphur Dioxide, Ozone, Aerosol Optical Depth, Dust, and UV Index. These parameters are used to assess the quality of air and calculate the corresponding AQI value.

## Analysis of Results

The generated AQI values range between approximately 26 and 69. Based on the AQI classification standards, values below 50 are categorized as **Good**, while values between 51 and 100 are categorized as **Moderate**.

The results show that a majority of the observations fall within the **Good** and **Moderate** air quality categories. AQI values such as 26.20, 27.04, 31.99, 38.94, and 42.36 indicate good air quality conditions. On the other hand, AQI values such as 54.31 and 69.42 fall under the moderate category, indicating acceptable air quality but with possible minor effects .

The agent  successfully converts complex pollutant measurements into a simple AQI value and category, making it easier for users to understand the current environmental conditions.
